## Introduction
This is an exploratory data analysis project on a global superstore data from https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/fatihilhan/global-superstore-dataset. The goal of this project is to analyze the data and discover performance trends across sales, profit, product categories and customer demographics. 

## Executive Summary
From 2011 to 2014, total sales reached 13M USD with a net profit of 1.47M USD, yielding an overall profit margin of 12%. Total sales and profit has both grown gradually across the evaluated period, while profit margin remained steady. Customer return rate is exceptionally high, where 99% of customers has made multiple orders. 68% of customers has made 10+ orders with this period, suggesting that a large proportion of customers are regulars.

## Product Analysis
The products are classified into 3 categories, including technology, furniture, and office supplies. Among all years, technology remains the highest selling category, taking 37.5% of overall sales. Phones and chairs are the highest selling sub-categories, gereating 17.3% and 15.3% of overall sales respectively. For top product, 87k Apple Smart Phone were sold. One notable change is that canon copiers had a surge in sales in 2013 and has seen rapid growth.

## Customer Demographic Analysis
Overall, the top selling and profiting country is United States, with a sales of $2M and a profit of $286k. The top performance city is New York City, with a sales of $256k and profit of $64k. In 2013, Los Angeles had a spike in sales and is the top selling city with $56k, but the top profiting city remains to be New York. France and Australia are the second and third selling countries, yet they face low profit margins. China and India surpasses them in profit made despite lower sales.
